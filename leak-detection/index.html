Local Context

Where Hidden Leaks Tend To Show Up In York

Older supply lines in pre-1960 homes, particularly galvanized pipe that's corroding from the inside, are a common source of slab and wall leaks that don't show up as an obvious drip right away. This is common across Valley Green, PA and the rest of York County too.

Signs To Watch For

Symptoms Of A Leak Detection Problem

Rising Water Bill With No Explanation

A bill that's climbed steadily without a change in household usage is one of the clearest signs of a hidden leak.

Warm Spot On The Floor

A warm patch on a slab floor, especially over a hot water line, is a classic sign of a slab leak.

Sound Of Running Water With Everything Off

Hearing water movement when no fixture is in use points to a leak somewhere in the system.

Damp Spot On A Wall Or Ceiling

A recurring damp patch with no obvious source often traces back to a slow leak inside the wall.

FAQs

Common Questions

How much does leak detection cost in York, PA?

Most electronic leak detection visits run $250 to $500 depending on the size of the home and how many areas need to be traced.

Can you find a leak without cutting into my walls?

In most cases, yes. Acoustic and electronic tracing narrows the location before any cutting happens, which is the whole point of the service.

What's a slab leak and how serious is it?

A slab leak is a leak in a water line running under or through a concrete foundation. It's serious because it can undermine the slab or cause water damage before it's found, which is why early detection matters.

How accurate is electronic leak detection?

It's highly accurate at narrowing a leak to a specific area, though the final confirmation happens visually once we open that specific spot.

Will detecting the leak also fix it?

Detection locates the leak. Repair is a separate step we'll quote once we know exactly what we're dealing with.
