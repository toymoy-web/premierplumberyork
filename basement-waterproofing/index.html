Basement waterproofing shows up in York's top search terms alongside boiler repair, and the reason traces back to Codorus Creek. Downtown and creek-adjacent neighborhoods have a real flood history, including the August 1933 storm and the 1972 Hurricane Agnes remnant flood, and even with post-1972 flood control work, older basements near the creek corridor still take on water during heavy regional rain. We install interior drainage and sump systems sized for that reality, not a generic waterproofing package.

Local Context

Codorus Creek And York's Basement Flood Risk

Indian Rock Dam and the stormwater pump stations completed in 1980 significantly reduced downtown York's flood exposure, but they did not eliminate it for every basement near the creek corridor. Older basement walls built before modern waterproofing membranes were standard practice are also more prone to hydrostatic pressure pushing water through the foundation during a wet spring thaw. This is common across Valley Green, PA and the rest of York County too.

Signs To Watch For

Symptoms Of A Basement Waterproofing Problem

Water Along The Floor-Wall Joint

Water seeping where the basement floor meets the foundation wall is the classic sign of hydrostatic pressure pushing groundwater in.

Efflorescence

White, chalky mineral deposits on foundation walls mean water has been moving through the concrete, even if it isn't pooling now.

Musty Basement Smell

A persistent damp smell with no visible water often means moisture is entering slowly enough to evaporate before you see it.

Sump Pump Running Constantly

A sump pump that cycles nonstop during rain is telling you the groundwater table around your foundation is genuinely high.

Our Approach

How We Waterproof A York Basement

We start with where the water is actually entering, not a generic sealant sales pitch. An interior drainage system routes water to a sump basin before it ever reaches the floor, and we size the sump pump and add a battery backup for storm-related power outages, which matter more in a creek-adjacent home than an inland one.

FAQs

Common Questions

How much does basement waterproofing cost in York, PA?

Most interior drainage and sump installations for a York basement fall between $2,800 and $7,500 depending on basement size and whether a sump system is already in place.

Will waterproofing stop water from Codorus Creek flooding?

Interior drainage and sump systems handle groundwater and storm infiltration well. Major creek overflow into a home is a different problem and may call for flood-specific measures, which we'll tell you honestly if that's what we're seeing.

Do I need a sump pump if I already have a French drain?

An interior drain system needs somewhere to send the water it collects. A sump pump is usually part of the same system, not a separate add-on.

How long does basement waterproofing take?

Most interior drainage and sump installations for a typical York basement take one to two days.

Does waterproofing add value if I plan to finish my basement?

Yes. Addressing water entry before finishing a basement protects the framing, drywall, and flooring you're about to install.

Can you waterproof an older stone foundation?

Yes, though older stone and rubble foundations common in York's historic core sometimes need a modified drainage approach, which we'll walk through on site.
