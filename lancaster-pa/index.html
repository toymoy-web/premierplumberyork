Overview

Plumbing In Lancaster, Pennsylvania

Lancaster sits about 26 miles east of York, reached primarily via US-30, and is the county seat of Lancaster County in its own right, separate from York County. It's one of the oldest inland cities in the United States, settled in the 1720s, and served as Pennsylvania's state capital from 1799 to 1812. We dispatch to Lancaster from our York base, and burst pipe repair is consistently the service homeowners there search for and call about first.
